ChoreQuest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

The short version: ChoreQuest stores only what is needed to run the app — your email address, your family's data, and your children's first names. We do not sell data, show ads, or share anything with third parties beyond the cloud services that power the app.

1. Who we are

ChoreQuest is an independent app developed by Chris Skaaning. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at c@rpediem.com.

2. What data we collect and why

ChoreQuest collects the minimum data needed to operate. The table below lists everything stored, where it lives, and why.

Data Where stored Why
Account email address Firebase Authentication Used to log in. No marketing emails are sent.
Family name Firebase Firestore Displayed in the app to identify the family.
Family join code Firebase Firestore A 6-character code that lets another adult join the same family.
Children's first names and chosen avatar Firebase Firestore Displayed in the app. Avatars are emoji-based presets — no photos are collected.
Quest (chore) names, icons, and settings Firebase Firestore The quests you create for your children.
Completion records Firebase Firestore Records of when a child marked a quest complete and whether a grown-up approved or rejected it. Includes the date (YYYY-MM-DD) and star count.
Star balance, rewards and reward requests Firebase Firestore Each child's star balance, the rewards you have created, and which child asked for which reward and whether it was approved or declined.
Device push token Firebase Firestore Stored when you grant notification permission. Used to send a push notification when a child completes a quest. Never used for marketing.
Grown-up PIN (hashed) Device only (expo-secure-store) A 4-digit PIN a grown-up enters to leave child mode. Stored only on the device as a SHA-256 hash; the PIN itself is never stored, and it never leaves the device.
Purchase receipt / entitlement RevenueCat servers Records whether you have purchased ChoreQuest Plus (unlimited children and rewards). Managed by RevenueCat — see their privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • Photos or camera access
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Advertising or cross-app tracking of any kind
  • Usage statistics or crash reports, unless you switch them on (see below)
  • Children's ages or dates of birth

Optional: anonymous usage statistics and crash reports

These are off unless you say yes. After you set up your family, the app asks once whether you'd like to help improve ChoreQuest. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Your data & privacy, and switching it off stops sending straight away.

If you switch it on, the app sends:

  • Anonymous usage counts to TelemetryDeck: the name of an action (for example "quest completed" or "reward requested") and, for a few actions, a coarse detail such as the icon chosen or a star-cost range. They are tied to a random number created on your device, which is reset when you switch this off or sign out. No names, email addresses, quest or reward text, account IDs or device identifiers are ever sent, and nothing at all about your children beyond the name of the action.
  • Crash reports to Sentry (EU data region): technical details of an error (app version, device model, the code location of the crash). Email addresses and database IDs are stripped before sending, and no user information is attached.

This data is used only to find bugs and to see which features are used. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or combined with data from other apps.

4. Children's privacy (COPPA / UK GDPR)

ChoreQuest is designed for use by parents, guardians and carers with young children. Children do not create accounts and do not provide any personal information to the app themselves. The only child data stored — first name and chosen avatar — is entered by the adult account holder.

We do not knowingly collect any personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

5. Cloud services we use

ChoreQuest is built on the following third-party services, each of which processes data as a data processor on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication + Firestore) — stores account credentials and all family data. Hosted in the EU/US depending on your region. See Firebase Privacy.
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and entitlements. See RevenueCat Privacy.
  • TelemetryDeck (only if you switch on usage statistics) — anonymous usage counts, processed in the EU. See TelemetryDeck Privacy.
  • Sentry (only if you switch on crash reports) — crash reports, stored in Sentry's EU data region. See Sentry Privacy.
  • Expo (push notifications) — push tokens are forwarded to Expo's push API when a quest completion notification is sent. See Expo Privacy.

We do not share your data with any other third parties, and none of these services are authorised to use your data for their own purposes.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains in Firestore for as long as you use the app. You can delete your account and all your family's data at any time inside the app: Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. If another adult shares the family, only your account is removed and the family stays with them. You can also email c@rpediem.com and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

Your Firebase Authentication account (email address) will be deleted at the same time. Push tokens are deleted when a device logs out.

7. Security

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest is protected by Firebase's built-in encryption. Access to your family's data in Firestore is enforced by security rules that require authentication — no other user can read or modify your family's data.

Grown-up PINs are hashed with SHA-256 and kept only on the device. Child mode runs under the account holder's account: children never need an email address, password or account of their own.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can do most of this yourself in the app: Settings → Download my data gives you a copy of everything stored about you and your family, you can edit names, quests and rewards at any time, and Settings → Delete account removes it all. If you can't sign in, or want help, email c@rpediem.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
