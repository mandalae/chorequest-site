Common questions

How does the reward shop work?

Stars are a balance. In the reward shop you add rewards with a title, star cost, picture and which children can get them. A child can pin one as Saving for to see a progress bar, and taps Get it! when they have enough stars. A grown-up then approves it (the stars are spent) or declines it. Kids can't spend stars without approval.

How do I approve a reward?

When a child taps Get it!, the request shows up for a grown-up. Open it and choose Approve to spend the stars, or Decline to keep them.

How do I unlock more children and rewards?

ChoreQuest is free with one child and one reward. Adding more shows a one-off purchase, ChoreQuest Plus (£4.99), which unlocks unlimited children and unlimited rewards. It's not a subscription and there are no ads. With Family Sharing it also covers another adult's Apple ID in your Family Sharing group. Existing purchases count.

I bought the unlock but it isn't showing

Open the paywall (try adding a second child) or go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same ChoreQuest account and Apple ID you bought it with.

How does another adult join?

Another adult, such as a partner, co-parent, grandparent or carer, creates their own account and enters your 6-character family code, which is shown in your family settings.

How does child mode work?

On the dashboard, tap Child mode for a child. The first time on each device you choose a 4-digit grown-up PIN. The device then shows only that child's quests, even after the app is restarted, until a grown-up taps Grown-ups and enters the PIN. It works the same on your own phone or on the child's own tablet (sign in there with your own account first).

How do I change my email or password?

Open Settings from the dashboard and choose to change your email or password. There you can also rename your family, share the family code, set the reminder time, change the grown-up PIN and restore purchases.

I forgot the grown-up PIN

Tap Grown-ups, then Forgot PIN? Use your account password. After entering your account password you can choose a new PIN.

How do I delete my account?

Dashboard → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and your family's data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
