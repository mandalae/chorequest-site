How it works

Three steps, no fuss.

  1. Create quests

    Add daily or weekly chores for each child and choose how many stars each is worth.

  2. Kids tick them off

    Your child opens their own simple view and marks quests as done.

  3. You approve

    Check the work and approve it. Stars build up in a balance, ready to spend in the reward shop.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Daily and weekly quests

Each quest is worth one to three stars. Set them once and they come back when they should.

Reward shop

Stars are a balance your child spends in a shop you stock. Add rewards with a title, star cost and picture, and choose which children can get them. Kids pin one as “Saving for” and see a progress bar. When they can afford it they tap “Get it!”, and you approve or decline. Nothing is spent without a grown-up saying yes.

Child mode with a parent PIN

Kids only see their own quests. A grown-up taps the Grown-ups button and enters the PIN to leave. Forgot the PIN? Use your parent password.

Two-parent families

Share a family with a 6-character family code. Both parents can set up quests and rewards, and approve them. Settings also lets you change your email or password, rename the family and set the reminder time.

Evening reminder

At 7pm the app reminds you to review completed quests.

Questions

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet.

Do my kids need their own device?

No. Child mode works on your phone, or on a tablet your child uses.

What if I forget the PIN?

Tap Grown-ups, then “Forgot PIN?”. After you enter your parent password you can choose a new PIN.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is free for one child and one reward, and ChoreQuest Plus is a single £4.99 purchase. No ads.

How does the reward shop work?

Stars are a balance. A parent adds rewards with a title, star cost, picture and which children can get them. A child can pin one as “Saving for” to see a progress bar, and taps “Get it!” when they have enough stars. A grown-up then approves it, which spends the stars, or declines it. Kids can’t spend stars without approval.

What do I get with ChoreQuest Plus?

A one-off £4.99 purchase that unlocks unlimited children and unlimited rewards. The free app has one child and one reward. It isn’t a subscription, there are no ads, and Apple Family Sharing covers the other parent. Existing purchases count.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account. See the privacy policy for details, or contact support.
